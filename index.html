Strong Suction

Zero Tangle 4.0

Patented V-shaped comb technology along with Cyclone Suction of dust ensures 0% Hair Tangling. Saves all your time of maintaining your product & keep the Deebot clean for next use.

TruEdge 3.0

Only brand to deliver 100% edge-to-edge cleaning, No dust line left. Its air-cushion roller glides along walls without scratching furniture.

SMART App control

Control your Ecovacs Robot from multiple devices, wherever you are in the world. Start the cleaning and come back to a cleaner home, even when you’re away.

AIVI 3D Obstacle Avoidance

Only Brand with most advanced AIVI 3D Obstacle Avoidance Technology, designed to detect even the smallest obstacle & intelligently navigate cleaning around them. Ensuring smoother, faster & effective cleaning.

OZMO Roller Mopping

Patented OZMO Roller Mopping delivers high pressure on hard floor to remove tough stains such as dry coffee/tea liquid, oil etc. It is Specially Designed for Indian house mopping preferences, delivering a powerful and effective cleaning experience.

All-in-One OMNI Station

Enjoy complete peace of mind. It auto-empties dust in dust bag, Re-fills clean water, Washes the mop pads & dries them with hot air for No Odor & Hygienic cleaning, ensuring better cleaning. Near Zero Maintenance & minimal human intervention.

Power Boost Technology
